Childlock Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026 · Last updated: June 10, 2026

Summary: Childlock helps a parent or guardian configure short brain breaks during selected apps on a child's device. We do not sell personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use child data for third-party advertising.

1. Who We Are

Childlock is developed and operated by Koubou Labs Pte Ltd. We can be reached at hello@kouboulabs.com.

2. What Childlock Does

Childlock uses Apple's Screen Time, Family Controls, DeviceActivity, and ManagedSettings frameworks to help parents and guardians pause selected apps and ask a child to complete a short challenge before returning to the app.

3. Information Stored On Device

Childlock stores child profiles, app selections, parent settings, challenge state, and Screen Time selection tokens locally on the device or in the app group shared with Childlock's extensions. Apple's app selection tokens are opaque and are not designed to reveal the child's full app list to us.

4. Information Synced

If account sync is enabled, Childlock may sync parent-owned account data, settings, subscription status, and summary challenge activity to our backend so the app can restore state and support purchases. Child names and Screen Time app selection token payloads are intended to remain local.

5. Purchases and Analytics

If paid features are offered, purchases are processed by Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat. We do not receive payment card details. Childlock may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand reliability and feature usage, but not for advertising to children.

6. Children's Privacy

Childlock is configured by a parent or guardian. Children do not create public profiles, post content, chat, or receive behavioral advertising in Childlock. We do not knowingly sell or share children's personal information.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may use service providers only to operate the app, process purchases, provide account sync, or respond to support requests. These providers are not permitted to use Childlock data for their own advertising.

8. Parental Controls and Deletion

A parent or guardian can remove child profiles, change monitored apps, stop Screen Time enforcement, or delete Childlock from the device. Deleting the app removes locally stored Childlock data from that device, subject to standard iOS backup and system behavior.

9. Apple Screen Time APIs

Childlock depends on Apple authorization and system frameworks. iOS settings, permissions, Family Sharing configuration, system updates, or entitlement changes may affect how enforcement works.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Childlock evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page and, when appropriate, communicating inside the app.
