Childlock Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026 · Last updated: May 8, 2026

Summary: Childlock is designed with children's privacy as a priority. Child data (names, challenge results, screen time) is stored entirely on-device and is never transmitted to our servers. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store guidelines for kids' apps.

1. Who We Are

Childlock is developed and operated by Kopi Koubou (DBA Koubou Labs), based in Tokyo, Japan. We can be reached at hello@kouboulabs.com.

2. What Childlock Does

Childlock is a parental control app for iOS that adds age-appropriate brain challenges (math, patterns, memory, puzzles) during children's screen time. Parents configure the app on their own device; challenges appear on the child's device via Apple's Family Sharing and Screen Time APIs.

3. Information We Collect

From Children (Ages 3-12)

We do not collect any personal information from children. All child-related data stays on the parent's device and the child's device. It is never sent to our servers.

From Parents

When parents create an account, we may collect:

  • Apple ID authentication token -- used for Sign in with Apple. We receive a unique identifier and optionally an email address. We do not receive the parent's Apple ID password.
  • Subscription status -- managed by Apple's App Store and RevenueCat. We receive subscription tier and validity, not payment details.
  • Parent PIN -- stored locally on-device using the iOS Keychain (encrypted). Never transmitted.

4. How We Use Information

  • Child data is used exclusively on-device to generate age-appropriate challenges, track progress, and display statistics to the parent.
  • Parent authentication data is used to manage the account and subscription.
  • We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, or marketing to children.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information with third parties, with these limited exceptions:

  • Apple -- processes authentication (Sign in with Apple) and subscriptions (App Store). Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat -- manages subscription status. Receives only anonymous app user IDs, not child data. Governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

No child data is shared with any third party under any circumstances.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • All child data is stored on-device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms (App Groups container, Keychain).
  • Data is encrypted at rest by iOS device encryption.
  • The parent PIN is hashed using SHA-256 before storage.
  • We do not operate backend servers that store child data.
  • Deleting the Childlock app removes all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Childlock is designed for use by parents and guardians to manage their children's screen time. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • No direct collection from children. Children interact only with challenge screens. There are no input fields, accounts, chat, or user-generated content on the child's device.
  • Parental consent. A parent or guardian must set up Childlock using their own device and Apple ID. Apple's Family Sharing mechanism serves as the verifiable parental consent method.
  • No behavioral tracking. We do not track children across apps or websites. Challenge usage data stays on the parent's device.
  • No advertising. Childlock contains no advertisements of any kind.

8. Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review all data associated with your child's profile (visible in the Childlock parent dashboard).
  • Delete your child's profile and all associated data at any time from the Children tab in the app.
  • Refuse further collection by removing Childlock from your child's device via Apple's Family Sharing settings.
  • Request information about our data practices by contacting us at hello@kouboulabs.com.

We will respond to all parental requests within 30 days.

9. Apple Screen Time APIs

Childlock uses Apple's FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity frameworks. These are Apple's official parental control APIs that:

  • Use opaque tokens to identify apps (we never see app bundle IDs or names).
  • Operate entirely on-device within Apple's security sandbox.
  • Require explicit parental authorization via Family Sharing.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or via the email associated with your Apple ID. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
